ChatGPT vs RAG

After learning about both ChatGPT and RAG, I understood that they are designed for different purposes. ChatGPT mainly answers questions using the knowledge it learned during training, so it works well for general topics like coding, writing, learning concepts, and brainstorming ideas. However, it may sometimes provide outdated information or generate incorrect answers, which is known as hallucination. On the other hand, RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) first searches for relevant information from external sources such as company documents, databases, PDFs, or websites and then uses that information to generate its response. Because of this, I feel that RAG provides more accurate and reliable answers, especially for company-specific or domain-specific questions. It also reduces the chances of hallucination since the response is based on retrieved documents instead of only the model's trained knowledge. From my understanding, ChatGPT is a great choice for general-purpose tasks, while RAG is more suitable for business applications such as customer support, HR assistants, healthcare, banking, legal systems, and company knowledge bases where accurate and up-to-date information is important. Overall, I think RAG is the better option whenever an AI system needs to answer questions using an organization's own data, whereas ChatGPT is more useful for everyday learning and productivity tasks.
