Project Proposal: PantryPal AI (Smart Recipe & Waste Optimizer)
--------------------------------------------------------------


1. Problem Statement
--------------------
Every week, households throw away lots of food because ingredients spoil before they can be used. People also experience "decision fatigue" when looking inside their fridge. They see a bunch of random ingredients (like half an onion, some yogurt, and pasta) but don't know how to turn them into a meal, leading to:
* Financial waste from thrown-out groceries.
* Environmental impact from food waste going to landfills.
* Stress and friction in planning daily meals.


2. Target Users
---------------
* Busy professionals and students who want quick recipe ideas using whatever is currently in their kitchen.
* Eco-conscious cooks looking to run a zero-waste household.
* Novice home cooks who need guidance and ingredient substitution ideas.


3. Proposed AI Solution
-----------------------
PantryPal AI is a mobile/web app that turns household ingredients into instant recipe ideas:
* Fridge Scanner: Snaps a photo of the fridge or pantry shelf. A vision model scans the image to identify ingredients (like carrots, chicken, or cheese) and flags items that look like they need to be used soon.
* Recipe Generator: Takes the ingredient list and creates a custom recipe, adapting to dietary preferences (like vegan, gluten-free, or low-carb).
* Smart Substitutions: Automatically updates recipes based on what's available (like substituting normal milk with almond milk).
* Minimalist Shopping Lists: If a user is missing a couple of items to make a recipe, the app generates a shopping list for only those missing ingredients.


4. Technologies to Use
----------------------
* User Interface: React Native (mobile app) or Next.js (web dashboard).
* Image & Text AI: Gemini 2.5 Flash. It supports vision (image inputs), is very fast, and is cost-efficient.
* Backend: Python using FastAPI to handle routing and calling the AI client.
* Database: PostgreSQL or SQLite to store user preferences and favorited recipes.
* Hosting: Google Cloud Run for hosting the backend application container.


5. Expected Benefits
--------------------
* Saves money by prioritizing ingredients before they expire.
* Cuts down on household food waste.
* Reduces daily stress about deciding what to cook.
* Easy to use by simply uploading a photo instead of typing out a long list.
